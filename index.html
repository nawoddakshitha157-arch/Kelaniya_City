Sacred ground touched by the Lord Buddha.

Kelaniya is a historic town on the northern bank of the Kelani River, about 10 km east of Colombo fort, western province Sri lanaka, with roots predating 500 BC when it was known as Kalyani Nagar.

History, Landmarks, and Culture

  • HistoryAccording to the Mahavamsa, The Lord Buddha visited Kelaniya in the 5th century BC to settle a dispute between two Naga chiefs, preaching from a gem studded throne later enshrined in the temple's stupa. The town later served as the capital of provincial king Kelani Tissa.
  • LandmarkKelaniya Raja Maha Viharaya, University of Kelaniya, Kelaniya New Bridge, Water World Kelaniya and Kelani River.
  • CultureThe Duruthu Maha Perahera is an annual grand Perahera held at the Kelaniya temple every January full moon, which celebrates the Lord Buddha's visit to Sri Lanka with decorated elephants, dancers, and drummers, thereby portraying traditional Buddhist Sinhalese culture.
  • GeographyBounded by the Kelani River; close enough to Colombo to sit within its urban belt.
